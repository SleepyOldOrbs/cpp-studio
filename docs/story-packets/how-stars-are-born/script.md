# Script: The Nursery of Stars

Subject: how stars are born

## Cast

- Narrator
- Nova
- Dr. Lumen

## Script

Narrator: In the dark between stars, the story begins inside a molecular cloud:
a huge, cold region of gas and dust. It is not empty. It is raw material waiting
for gravity to find a place to gather. [fact-1, fact-2]

Nova: So a star does not begin as a spark. It begins as a cloud? [fact-1]

Dr. Lumen: Exactly. The cloud is cold enough for gas to clump. When one pocket
becomes dense enough, its gravity grows stronger, and the pocket starts to
collapse inward. [fact-2, fact-3]

Narrator: The collapse does not make just one simple ball all at once. The cloud
can fragment into smaller star-forming pockets, each with its own local pull of
gravity. [fact-3]

Nova: What do we call the baby star in the middle? [fact-4]

Dr. Lumen: A protostar. It is not yet a stable main-sequence star. It is still a
growing object, fed by gas from the cloud around it. [fact-4, fact-5]

Narrator: Material keeps falling inward, but it does not fall like rain in a
straight line. Rotation makes the gas spiral around the center, spreading into a
disk. [fact-6]

Nova: That disk is not decoration, then. It is part of the engine. [fact-6]

Dr. Lumen: Right. The accretion disk feeds the protostar. Bit by bit, material
crosses inward and adds mass to the forming star. [fact-7]

Narrator: As the protostar gathers mass, the center compresses. Pressure and
temperature rise, pushing the core closer to the conditions needed for stable
nuclear fusion. [fact-8]

Nova: But if everything is spinning, why does the gas not just whirl forever?
[fact-6, fact-10]

Dr. Lumen: Young stars can launch jets from near their magnetic poles. Those
jets help carry away angular momentum, which lets more material keep collecting
instead of only circling. [fact-9, fact-10]

Narrator: A star is born through cooperation between cold gas, gravity, spin,
disks, jets, and heat. The cloud gathers. The pocket collapses. The protostar
feeds. The core compresses. [fact-1, fact-3, fact-5, fact-8]

Nova: And the final moment is fusion? [fact-8]

Dr. Lumen: Stable fusion is the threshold that changes the story. The object is
no longer just a growing protostar. It has become a star. [fact-8]

Narrator: What looked like darkness was a nursery. What looked like dust was a
future sun. [fact-1, fact-4]
